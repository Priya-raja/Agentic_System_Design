# How much baggage comes with my fare

Economy Saver includes 20 kg checked baggage, Economy Flex 25 kg, and Business 40 kg total. Gold members may receive 5 kg more on eligible AeroNova operated flights. Each individual checked bag must stay at or below 32 kg. Always check the ticket for booking-specific allowances.
