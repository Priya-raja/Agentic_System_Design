# Can I upgrade my AeroNova ticket to Business?

Business upgrade eligibility depends on the cabin shown on the original ticket.

- Economy Saver and Economy Flex tickets cannot be upgraded directly to Business.
- Premium Economy Standard tickets may be upgraded to Business when an eligible upgrade offer is available.
- A passenger originally booked in Economy cannot first upgrade to Premium Economy Standard and then upgrade again to Business on the same journey.

An empty Business seat does not necessarily mean upgrade inventory is available. Group fares, staff travel, infant tickets without a separate seat, and bookings with unresolved ticketing or payment issues are excluded.

The passenger remains in the original cabin until AeroNova issues an upgrade confirmation. Codeshare flights operated by another airline follow that carrier's rules.
