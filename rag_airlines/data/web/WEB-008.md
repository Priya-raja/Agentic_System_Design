# How do I redeem my AeroNova points?
This is fictional test material. See POL-REWARDS-2026 and POL-REDEEM-2026 for the controlling rules.

Sign in to Rewards, choose Redeem points, search your route and date, select an available offer, review the points and separate taxes, then confirm. Public policy explains the steps; your balance and available offers require a secure account session.

Reward flights start at 10,000 points in Economy and 35,000 points in Business one way. These are minimums, not live quotes. Selected points-plus-cash offers start at 5,000 points, using the rate in the offer. Seat availability does not guarantee reward inventory.

Cash and points upgrades follow the same original-cabin restrictions. An Economy Flex ticket cannot upgrade directly to Business. Standard reward cancellations before departure return points less a 2,000-point fee under the policy; promotional and post-departure cases need their own rules checked.
