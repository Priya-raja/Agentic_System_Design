# AeroNova Rewards programme rules
Document ID: POL-REWARDS-2026 | Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 | Status: active
FICTIONAL TEST POLICY. All names, points amounts and rules are invented for this project.

## Earning points
Members earn 5 redeemable points per AED 1 of eligible base fare paid on AeroNova-operated flights. Taxes, government fees, ancillary purchases and the portion paid with points do not earn points. Points post within seven days after the flown segment. Cancelled flights and unused tickets earn no flight points. Eligible activity requires the membership number on the booking and matching passenger identity.

## Missing points
A member can submit a missing-points request between 7 days and 180 days after travel with the ticket, boarding pass and membership number. An authenticated rewards service must check the flight and account before crediting points. The public assistant cannot inspect balances or credit an account.

## Expiry and tiers
Redeemable points expire after 24 months without an eligible earn or redemption transaction. Logging in or requesting a balance does not reset expiry. Promotional points may have shorter expiry printed in the offer. Tier credits are separate from redeemable points: redeeming points does not increase status. Silver requires 10,000 tier credits and Gold requires 25,000 tier credits in a calendar year; status lasts through the end of the following calendar year. Tier benefits and exclusions are controlled by the relevant baggage and fare policies.

## Account security
Balances, pending credits, expiry dates for an individual account and transaction history require authentication. Never disclose another member's balance. Points are not cash, cannot be sold, and have no guaranteed monetary value. General earning and redemption rules can be answered from public policy without authentication.
