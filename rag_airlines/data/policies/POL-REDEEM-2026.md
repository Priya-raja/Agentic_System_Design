# Redeeming AeroNova Rewards points
Document ID: POL-REDEEM-2026 | Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 | Status: active
FICTIONAL TEST POLICY. These are invented rules, not actual airline offers.

## How to redeem
Sign in to the secure AeroNova Rewards account, select Redeem points, search for an AeroNova-operated flight and choose an available reward offer. Review the points, taxes, fees and fare conditions before confirming. The points balance is debited only after successful confirmation. The public-policy assistant explains these steps but cannot log in, redeem points, quote live prices or guarantee availability.

## Reward flights
Economy reward flights start at 10,000 points one way and Business reward flights start at 35,000 points one way. These are illustrative minimums, not guaranteed quotes. Route, date, cabin and reward inventory determine the actual points price. Taxes and fees are payable separately in money. Reward inventory is separate from ordinary seat inventory. An empty seat does not prove reward availability. Waitlisted or unconfirmed requests do not create a ticket.

## Points plus cash
Selected offers allow points plus cash with a minimum redemption of 5,000 points. The points-to-cash rate is shown in the live offer and is not a fixed conversion. A signed-in workflow must check the balance and offer before committing a redemption. Never infer that a member has sufficient points from a public policy document.

## Upgrades with points
Points upgrades remain subject to POL-UPG-2026. Economy Saver and Economy Flex tickets cannot upgrade directly to Business. An originally Economy ticket cannot bypass this rule by upgrading sequentially through Premium Economy. Eligible originally Premium Economy Standard tickets may upgrade to Business only when an eligible offer and upgrade inventory are available. A points balance alone never establishes eligibility.

## Changes and cancellations
Reward fare conditions are displayed before confirmation. For this fictional standard reward product, cancellation before departure returns redeemed points less a 2,000-point cancellation fee, never taking the balance below zero; refundable taxes return to the original payment method. Points retain their original expiry date; expired points are not revived. After departure or a no-show, points are not automatically returned and review is required. Promotional reward offers may have different disclosed terms. A specific cancellation or points refund requires authentication and a transaction workflow.
